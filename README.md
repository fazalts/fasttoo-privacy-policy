# Privacy Policy — fasttoo Variation Editor

Last updated: 27 October 2026

**Operator:** fasttoo ltd, United Kingdom  
**Privacy contact:** baooukltd@gmail.com

## 1. Information we process

We process account identifiers, email addresses and verification status needed for sign-in; connected eBay seller identifiers and encrypted authorisation credentials; listing information used for previews and verification; and edit history, credit balances, trial eligibility, credit requests and related administrative records.

We also process authentication cookies and technical request/security logs. Information comes from you, our sign-in provider and the eBay account you authorise.

Existing variation-picture references may be processed temporarily to preserve their associations. This is not a photo-upload or photo-management service.

## 2. Why we use information

We use information to provide the service you request: managing access, connecting your seller account, fetching listings, preparing previews, submitting confirmed changes and checking their outcomes.

We also use information for our legitimate interests in securing the service, preventing misuse and repeated free-trial claims, maintaining accurate credit records and investigating uncertain edit outcomes.

Account and eBay authorisation information are necessary for the corresponding features. Without them, those features cannot operate.

## 3. Providers and access

We use Clerk for authentication and Replit for hosting and database infrastructure. We exchange information with eBay to perform authorised account connections, listing lookups, confirmed revisions and account-closure processing.

Authorised administrators can access account, activity and credit information needed to operate and support the service.

Providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Contact us for information about processing locations and applicable transfer arrangements.

## 4. Cookies and security

Authentication and session cookies support sign-in and account connections. Blocking them may prevent these features from working.

eBay authorisation credentials are encrypted, and application access is restricted by account and role. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Retention and eBay account closure

Retention depends on whether information remains necessary for an active account, a connection, an unresolved edit, a credit balance, security or abuse prevention. We do not promise an arbitrary deletion period that the service cannot enforce.

Following a verified eBay account-closure notification, we remove the affected local eBay connection credentials and associated listing details. This does not delete listings on eBay.

The application login and paid-credit balance may remain. Uncertain edit outcomes and reserved credits are held for administrator review rather than automatically retried, charged or refunded. Limited trial-history and keyed account-suppression records may remain to prevent repeated trials or restoration of a closed connection.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can disconnect your eBay account in the app or revoke access through eBay. Disconnecting does not automatically erase all historical records.

Depending on applicable law and the processing involved, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information.

**Right to object:** You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Send requests to baooukltd@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk.

Trial eligibility is checked automatically against the application account and connected eBay identity. Contact us if you believe a decision is incorrect.
Optional Google Analytics and advertising measurement
With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use Variation Editor and measure selected actions, such as completed registrations and eBay account connections. Measurement may include device and browser information, general location derived from IP address, page categories and interaction events. Google may use cookies and similar technologies for these purposes.

If you separately consent to advertising measurement, we also use the Google Ads tag to help measure advertising effectiveness. Our current implementation does not enable personalised advertising or enhanced conversions. Purchase-conversion tracking is not currently configured.

Our application is designed not to include your email address, eBay listing IDs, authorisation credentials or private dashboard URLs in the analytics events it sends.

Your choice: Optional Google tracking does not load until you give the relevant consent. You can reject optional tracking or withdraw consent at any time through Cookie preferences on our website. Refusing tracking does not prevent you from signing in, connecting your eBay account or purchasing credits. Withdrawing consent stops future tracking but does not automatically delete information already collected.

Google processes measurement information and may process it outside the United Kingdom. For information about Google’s processing, retention and international-transfer arrangements, see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services. Retention also depends on the settings applied to our Google measurement services.

We rely on your consent for optional analytics and advertising cookies and the associated measurement described above. You can contact us using the privacy contact listed in this policy to ask questions or exercise your data-protection rights.



## 7. Children and policy changes

The service is intended for adult sellers and authorised business users, not children. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been submitted.

Changes to this policy will be published on the policy page with an updated date.
